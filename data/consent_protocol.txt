Study Title: Evaluation of AI-Generated Content Summaries

Researcher: Qing Zhang
Institution: The University of Tokyo
Contact Email: qingzhang@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp

1. Introduction and Purpose
You are invited to participate in a research study aimed at understanding how users evaluate information presented in different digital interfaces. Specifically, we are investigating how people perceive the accuracy of summaries generated by artificial intelligence.

2. Procedures
If you agree to participate, you will be asked to:

Read three brief summaries (approximately 150–200 words each) regarding various scientific, historical, or technological topics.
Evaluate the accuracy and veracity of the information presented using a sliding scale.
Complete a brief demographic questionnaire.
Complete one attention check to ensure data quality.
The total time commitment is estimated to be approximately 8 minutes.

3. Participation and Withdrawal
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You are free to withdraw from the study at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled. If you choose to withdraw, simply close your browser window.

4. Compensation
Upon successful completion of the survey and validation of the attention check, you will receive payment via Prolific at the rate advertised (approximately £1.0).

5. Risks and Benefits
There are no known risks associated with this study beyond those encountered in everyday internet use. While there are no direct benefits to you, your participation will help researchers improve the transparency and design of AI systems.

6. Confidentiality and Data Privacy
All data collected is anonymous. We do not collect names, IP addresses, or any other personally identifiable information. Your Prolific ID will be used solely for the purpose of processing payment and will be stored separately from your survey responses. De-identified data may be shared in open-science repositories for the purpose of peer review and academic transparency.

7. Contact Information
If you have any questions about this research or your rights as a participant, please contact the lead researcher at qingzhang@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp.
